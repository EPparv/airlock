OPEN. IMPORT. CONNECT.

Your context.
Ready for your AI.

The Mac app handles installation for you. Use your existing AI account for answers, with selected context from Airlock.

  1. Double-click Airlock.

    Unzip the download and move Airlock.app to Applications. Open it. First launch installs its runtime automatically, then opens the local control panel in your browser. Internet is needed for setup.

  2. Bring your context.

    Paste memories, choose an export or connect local memory. Review what you import. Gmail is optional; new Gmail sign-in is not configured in this public build.

  3. Connect your AI.

    Choose Codex, Claude or Cursor. Airlock saves the connection settings. Restart your AI, enable Airlock if prompted, and use the test question. Keep Airlock open while using it.
